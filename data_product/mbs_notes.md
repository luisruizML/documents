# Reasons for Receiving a Benefit   

## Retirement
• Age benefit  

##  Incapacity to work shot-term
• Sickness benefit: due to a general illness or non-work-related injury
• Injury benefit: due to an injury sustained on the job (employment injury)
• Maternity & Paternity benefit   

##  Incapacity to work long-term / permanently
• Invalidity benefit: due to a general illness or injury (non-work related).
• Disablement benefit: for a physical or mental impairment resulting from an injury or disease sustained on the job (employment injury).   

## Death
• Funeral benefit: A specific one-time payment to whoever pays for the funeral of an insured person.
• Survivor benefit: Paid when an insured person dies from natural causes or non-work-related accidents, based on their accumulated contributions.
• Death benefit: paid specifically when a person dies as a result of a work-related accident or disease (employment injury)   

## Other:
• Constant Attendance benefit: For disabled persons who require constant personal care from another person to perform basic daily activities.
• Medical Expense benefit: In case of employment injury only.
• Unemployment benefit   


*Payable until death or until you reach retirement age.
